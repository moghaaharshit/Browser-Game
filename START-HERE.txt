==========================================================
  NEON CITY DRIVE  -  KAISE KHOLEIN (How to open)
==========================================================

1) Godot 4 install karein:      https://godotengine.org/download
   -> "Godot Engine" (standard version) download karein, .NET wala nahi.

2) Is ZIP ko extract karein. Aapko ek folder milega:
        neon-city-drive\

3) Godot kholein. Start window me "Import" par click karein.
   "Browse" karke us folder ke ANDAR ki file chunein:
        neon-city-drive\project.godot
   Phir "Import & Edit" dabayein.

   (Agar pehle se project khula hai: Project > Import > project.godot)

4) Editor khulne ke baad keyboard par F5 dabayein (ya top-right
   ka Play button) -> game start ho jayega.

----------------------------------------------------------
  CONTROLS
----------------------------------------------------------
  W / Arrow Up      : accelerator
  S / Arrow Down    : brake, phir reverse
  A / D             : steering
  Shift             : boost (nitro)
  Space             : handbrake (drift ke liye)
  V  (ya C)         : CAMERA BADLEIN - 3 angle hain:
                        1) CLOSE     = car ke bilkul paas, tight chase cam
                        2) SPEED     = jitna fast jaoge utna peeche + wide lens
                        3) CINEMATIC = slow drone jo car ke chaaro taraf
                                       ghoomta hai (screenshot ke liye best)
  M                 : BADA MAP kholo / band karo
  F                 : CAR ME BAITHO / CAR SE UTRO
  MOUSE WHEEL / +/- : bade map ko zoom in / zoom out karo
  R                 : wapas road par respawn
  L                 : shadows on/off (performance badhane ke liye)
  Esc               : game band karein

----------------------------------------------------------
  PAIDAL (ON FOOT) - GAME YAHIN SE SHURU HOTI HAI
----------------------------------------------------------
  Aap gaadi ke paas khade ho. F dabao to gaadi me baith
  jaoge, wapas F dabao to utar jaoge.

  W A S D           : chalna
  SHIFT             : sprint (tez daud)
  CTRL              : crouch (baith ke chalna)
  Z                 : PRONE - zameen par let ke crawl karna
  SPACE             : JUMP
  LEFT CLICK        : GUN SE FIRE (hold = drag fire, lagataar chalti
                        rahegi). Haath me grenade/gloo/medkit ho to
                        ek click me wahi use hota hai.
  RIGHT CLICK       : AIM - har gun par GOL CHAKKAR WALI SIGHT khulti
                        hai (rifle/SMG/pistol par laal dot ring,
                        KAR98K/AWM par poora zoom wala sniper scope)
  TAB (dabaye rakho): WEAPON WHEEL - GTA 5 jaisa gol chakkar. Mouse
                        ghumao, jis hisse par mouse ho wahi select, TAB
                        chhodo to wo hath me aa jayega. Wheel me 10
                        gun + GRENADE + GLOO WALL + MEDKIT sab hai.
  E                 : saamne pada LOOT uthao (gun, vest, helmet,
                        grenade, gloo wall, medkit)
  K                 : medkit use karo (health full)
  G                 : gloo wall rakho (ek saath sirf 3 tikte hain)
  H                 : grenade phenko
  X                 : PUNCH - 3 hit ka combo
  MOUSE             : camera ghumao (dekhne ke liye)
  R                 : reload (gaadi me: road par respawn)
  V                 : walk camera paas / door

  NOTE: FIRE KARTE WAQT AIM KHUD UPAR NAHI JATA. Recoil sirf haath
  ko hilaata hai aur crosshair failata hai - bandook wahi rehti hai
  jahan aapka mouse hai.

  GLOO WALL AUTO REFILL: gloo wall rakhne ke 10 second baad aapka
  stock apne aap wapas 5 ho jaata hai (HUD me ulta countdown dikhta
  hai, jaise "7s"). Isliye cover hatne ke baad intezaar karo.

  HAATH ME KYA HAI WAHI DIKHEGA:
   - gun select -> dono haath gun par (daayan haath grip par,
     baayan haath hand guard par), gun chalne layak upar utthi hui
   - grenade select -> haath me grenade
   - gloo wall select -> haath me neela canister
   - medkit select -> haath me laal cross wala kit

  FIRE KARNE PAR PLAYER GHUMPTA HAI: aap kisi taraf khade ho,
  mouse ghumake doosri taraf aim kiya aur fire kiya -> fire karte
  hi pura character usi taraf ghoom jaata hai (peeth se goli nahi
  chalegi).

  JUMP me haath AAGE upar jaate hain (peeshe nahi), ghutne
  mukde hue - aur gun haath me ho to jump me bhi dono haath gun
  par hi rehte hain.

  Animation sab REAL-TIME bante hain (koi video clip nahi):
   - khade rehne par saans lena, idhar-udhar dekhna
   - chalne par haath-pair ka pura jhoola, kamar ka hilna
   - sprint me aage jhukna, tez kadam
   - crouch, prone crawl, jump (hawas me pair sametna)
   - punch par kamar ghoomna + guard hand upar

----------------------------------------------------------
  GHAR KE ANDAR JA SAKTE HO  +  ANDAR LOOT PADA HAI
----------------------------------------------------------
  AB POORE MAP KE HAR GHAR AUR HAR SHOP KE ANDAR JA SAKTE HO -
  aur har ek building ke andar RANDOM ITEMS pade milenge
  (100% ghar me - koi ghar khaali nahi):
    gun + uski ammo, body vest, helmet, grenade,
    gloo wall, medkit
  Item zameen par ghoomta hua chamakta hai; paas jaao to
  screen par [E] SCAR AMMO jaisa likha aata hai - E dabao,
  item aapka. Medkit K se use hota hai, gloo/grenade ka
  stock bhi loot se badhta hai.

  Ghar ke andar:
   - darwaza khula hai (leaf andar ki taraf khula hua)
   - charo taraf khidki, frame + glass + sill ke saath
   - ground floor par sofa, table, kursi, TV, bookshelf,
     kitchen counter, fridge, dining table, ceiling light
   - ANDAR SE SEEDI upar - 2nd floor me bed, wardrobe,
     desk, nightstand
   - doosri seedi se CHHAT par - chhat par water tank,
     AC unit, bench aur pura town dikhta hai
   - ghar ke andar apne aap warm light jal jati hai
   - ground floor aur upar ke floor par loot pade milenge

----------------------------------------------------------
  RED DRUM (BARREL) - BLAST HOTA HAI
----------------------------------------------------------
  Town aur factory area me laal drum (peeli patti wale)
  khade milenge:
   - PUNCH maro ya CAR se takkar do -> BLAST

  SCREEN PAR KYA DIKHEGA

  1) NEECHE DAAYEIN (bottom right) : ROUND SPEEDOMETER -
     needle wala, jo speed dikhata hai (sirf gaadi me).
     Paidal chalte waqt ye chhup jata hai.
  2) UPAR DAAYEIN (top right) : chhota SQUARE 3D MAP -
     asli city ka upar se dikhne wala view, sirf ~260 meter
     ka area - matlab apni lane, agla chauraha, ghar, ped,
     traffic light sab saaf dikhte hain (door ka overview nahi).
     Beech me neela ARROW = aapki car.
     MAP AAPKE SAATH GHUMPTA HAI - jis taraf car chal rahi
     hai wo hamesha UPAR rehta hai, aur N (north) ka nishan
     bhi ghoomta hai.

  BADA MAP (M dabao):
   - asli 3D duniya ka upar se bada view (flat naksha nahi)
   - saari 18 city ke naam dikhte hain, har city ke charo
     taraf ek rangeen gol nishan (jis city me aap ho uska
     naam white me chamakta hai)
   - YE BHI CHALTE WAQT GHUMPTA HAI - car jis taraf mudi,
     map usi taraf ghoom jata hai (thoda smooth lagega)
   - ZOOM: MOUSE WHEEL ghumao ya = / - dabao
       300 m  -> ek gali bhar
       8.6 km -> poora island
     kitna zoom hai wo upar likha hua aata hai (ZOOM 1.5 km)
   - MAP PAR KISI BHI JAGAH CLICK karo -> wahi aapki
     DESTINATION set ho jayegi
   - aapke arrow se us jagah tak PEELI DOTTED LINE dikhegi
     (distance bhi likha aayega)
   - agar destination screen ke bahar ho to line map ke
     kinare par ek DIAMOND nishan tak aati hai, taaki pata
     rahe kis taraf jaana hai
   - wahan pahunchte hi (30 meter ke andar) line gayab ho
     jayegi
   - M ya Esc dabao to map band

----------------------------------------------------------
  GAADIYAN - 8 ALAG TYPES (sirf ek jaisi gaadi nahi)
----------------------------------------------------------
  Poore island par 166 gaadiyan khadi hain, aur ab wo 8 ALAG
  TYPES ki hain - har type ka apna size, pahiye, aur apna
  handling:

   1) SEDAN   - aam gaadi (city ki sadkon par sabse zyada)
   2) SPORTS  - neechi, chaudi, badi spoiler wali (sabse tez)
   3) SUV     - unchi 4x4, roof rail
   4) VAN     - lambi panel van, peeche do darwaze
   5) PICKUP  - aage cab, peeche khula cargo bed
   6) JEEP    - chhoti, roll cage, snorkel, peeche spare wheel
   7) MONSTER TRUCK - 0.88 m ke bahut bade pahiye, uncha
                 chassis, roll cage, do chrome exhaust stack
   8) BIKE    - do pahiye, tank, fork, silencer - aur ek RIDER
                 baitha hua (bike akeli nahi chalti dikhti)

  - Kahan kaunsi milegi: city ki sadkon par sedan/van/bike,
    khuli country road par pickup/SUV/jeep, aur 115 meter se
    zyada unchi pahaadi road par JEEP aur kabhi MONSTER TRUCK.
  - Har type ka top speed, acceleration, steering, grip aur
    brake alag hai - monster truck bhaari aur slow, bike
    halki aur tez, sports sabse tez.
  - 10 rosh chamkile rang (laal, neela, peela, hara, safed,
    orange, purple, cyan, pink) badalte rehte hain.

  PAHIYE (TYRE) DETAIL:
  - har tyre 16 tread blocks + sidewall + alloy rim + hub +
    5 lug nuts se bana hai - paas jaake dekho to asli tyre
    lagta hai, kaala gol tukda nahi
  - chalte waqt tyre apni asli speed se ghoomta hai
    (wheel ke radius ke hisaab se), aage ke pahiye mudte hain
  - bike ka fork mudta hai aur bike curve me andar ki taraf
    jhukti hai

  ZAMEEN SOLID HAI - GAADI ZAMEEN ME NAHI GHUSTI:
  - pahaad ka asli collider hai, aur gaadi ka floor angle
    62 degree tak chalta hai (tez dhaal wali pahaadi road par
    bhi gaadi sadak par hi rehti hai)
  - har tick par gaadi ke neeche seedha ray jaata hai aur
    island ke height se cross-check hota hai - agar body
    kabhi zameen ke andar chali jaye to turant upar utha di
    jaati hai (velocity bhi zero)
  - body ke baad pahiye apni jagah par set hote hain:
    naali/khadda ho to tyre andar nahi ghusta, pahaad par
    tyre hawa me bhi nahi latakta (measure kiya: 5 mm se
    zyada galti nahi)

  Kisi bhi gaadi ke paas jao, F dabao -> aap us gaadi me
  baith jaoge. Wapas F -> utar jao. Sab gaadiyan asli
  physics wali hain, koi dummy nahi.

----------------------------------------------------------
  ZAMEEN AUR PAHAAD AB ZYADA DETAILED
----------------------------------------------------------
  - Zameen par ab ek sookshma daane (grain) wala texture hai
    (procedural noise, triplanar - matlab pahaad par chipka
    hua nahi lagta, har taraf natural lagta hai)
  - Poore island par (city ke bahar) PED, BOULDER (bade
    patthar) aur JHAADIYAN ug gayi hain: 48 meter ke tiles
    me, aapke 180 meter ke andar hi bante hain, aur teen
    MultiMesh me - matlab pura pahaad sirf 3 draw call
  - Ye kabhi sadak par ya ghar ke andar nahi ugte, aur dhaal
    par thoda zameen me dhase rehte hain taaki hawa me na
    latkein
  - Pahaad par snow line ~254 m, uske neeche chattanein,
    scree, ghaas ki paryaten, nadi ke kinare keechad - sab
    pehle se hai, ab uske upar ped-patthar bhi hai

----------------------------------------------------------
  NADIYAN, JHEELEIN, SAMUNDAR
----------------------------------------------------------
  Island par 9 nadiyan hain jo pahaadiyon se nikal ke
  samundar ki taraf behti hain (apna paani le kar, ghat ke
  saath). Kuch jagah jheelein bhi ban gayi hain. Paani ke
  paas jaake dekho:
   - nadi ka paani BEHTA hua dikhta hai (shader me do
     chalte hue sine wave - rang gehre teal se halke
     firozi tak badalta hai, dhoop me chamakta hai)
   - kinare par geele keechad aur ret ke patte dikhte hain
   - pahaadon par ab CRAG noise hai - tooti hui chattanein,
     naali jaisi erosion lines, patthar ki parat (strata),
     scree aur barf ki line (~254 m se upar barf, kinare
     noise se toote hue, khadi chattan par barf nahi rukti)
   - pahaadi ka peak 310 meter hai

----------------------------------------------------------
  SAB KUCH SOLID HAI
----------------------------------------------------------
  - Pahaad, zameen, har building ki deewar, ped ka tana,
    patthar, bench, street light, khadi car, bus, drum,
    bot - sab par asli collision hai.
  - Pahaad ke andar nahi ghus sakte: upar chadhoge, andar
    nahi. Agar kabhi niche chale jao to game aapko wapas
    upar khada kar deta hai.
  - HAR ghar ke andar ja sakte ho (pehle 3 me se 4 the,
    ab 4 me se 4). Dukaan (commercial building) ka ground
    floor bhi asli kamra hai - darwaza, frame, khidki,
    awning, andar counter aur shelf.

----------------------------------------------------------
  MOBILE / PHONE CONTROLS (naya)
----------------------------------------------------------
Touch screen wale phone/tablet par apne aap on ho jate hain
(desktop par chhup jate hain). F9 se on/off kar sakte ho.

  * MOVE STICK (neeche baayen)  : jahan ungli rakho wahi stick
      ban jata hai, aadha push = aadhi speed (analogue).
  * FIRE (neeche daayen)        : dabao = goli, aur dabaye
      hue UNGLI KO GHIISKO = aim ghumega (Free Fire jaisa
      drag-fire). Ek hi ungli se fire + aim.
  * SPRINT / JUMP / CROUCH / RELOAD / CAR (gadi me baithna).
  * BAG (upar beech me)         : weapon wheel khulti hai.
      Ek tap = wheel khuli, phir weapon par tap = haath me.
      Bahar tap = band.
  * CAM = camera badlo, MAP = bada map (ungli se chalao).
  * Screen ke daayen hisse me kahin bhi drag = look/turn.

GAADI ME: wahi stick ab throttle + steering hai (upar =
speed, neeche = brake, baayen/daayen = steer) aur daayen
cluster BOOST / BRAKE / EXIT ban jata hai.

----------------------------------------------------------
  GITHUB PAR DEPLOY KARNA (link se khelo)
----------------------------------------------------------
1) GitHub par naya repo banao (khali).
2) Is folder me terminal kholein aur EK command chalao:
     ./tools/deploy_github.sh https://github.com/<naam>/<repo>.git

   (ya haath se:  git init -b main
                  git add .
                  git commit -m "Neon City Drive"
                  git remote add origin https://github.com/<naam>/<repo>.git
                  git push -u origin main )
3) Repo me: Settings -> Pages -> Build and deployment ->
   Source = "GitHub Actions" chunein.
4) Actions tab me build chalega (~3 min). Ready hone par:
     https://<naam>.github.io/<repo>/
   Par phone ya laptop, kuch install kiye bina khel lo.

Uska workflow file pehle se hai: .github/workflows/deploy.yml
(Apne aap Godot 4.7.2 + export template download karta hai.)

me chala kar dekha - engine start, pura island ban, town render,

----------------------------------------------------------
  THIS UPDATE - ZAMEEN AUR PAHAADI ROAD (fix)
----------------------------------------------------------
* "Gaadi zameen ke andar chali jati hai" - asli wajah road
  thi, car nahi. Pahaadi road 50 degree tak chadh rahi thi
  aur pahaad road ke upar se ubak raha tha (har road ka
  24% hissa pahaad ke andar tha, kabhi 20 meter tak).
  Ab:
  - road ka profile smooth hai aur dhalan 12% tak hi.
  - road ke aas paas zameen kat-ti/bharti hai: tarmac ke
    niche 30 cm jagah, aur 15 meter me pahaad wapas
    normal. Ab ghaas road ke upar nahi nikalti aur road
    ke niche chhupa pahaad gaadi ko nahi rokta.
  - har road aur har shehar ke tarmac par asli collision
    hai - jo aapko dikhta hai, wahi gaadi ka support hai.
    Tyre theek tarmac par rehta hai (0.00 meter error).
* Kerb / road ka kinara / shehar ke plate ka kinara 2-6 cm
  uncha hota hai - ab gaadi us par chadh jati hai, rukti
  nahi (34 cm tak step assist). Shehar me bahar se andar
  full speed par ghus sakte ho.
* GRAVITY ASLI HAI (9.81 m/s2) - car me bhi, paidal bhi.
  20 meter ki unchai se gaadi 2.02 second me girti hai.
* Paidal chalne ki speed bhi asli:
     chalna 2.6 m/s, daudna 6.4 m/s (23 km/h).
     jump 4.6 m/s -> 1.08 meter uncha, 0.94 second hawa me.
  Pehle yeh sab 2-3 guna tez tha, isliye "floaty" lagta tha.
